## Levers and switches (yours; every feature defaults on)

- `ZOE_TONE_TAG=0` (tones ignored), `ZOE_NONVERBAL=0` (no clips), `ZOE_FACE_SENSE=0` (no camera reading), `ZOE_REACH=0` (no reach, no timer).
- Meta: `camera.describe` (`0` turns the cloud expression read off; on by your word tonight, every use logged), `camera.describe_every_ms` (20000), `avatar.gaze_flip` (`1` mirrors her gaze if it goes the wrong way), `voice.state_baseline` (`1` lets her energy shift her speed by ±0.05), `reach.social_floor` (0.5), `reach.min_gap_ms` (5 h), `reach.max_unanswered` (2), `reach.answer_window_ms` (45 min).
- Privacy in code: the camera is on by default but never secret (the on-air dot) and yours to turn off (📷, persisted); no frame is ever written; other faces read as "someone else" and are never identified; the meeting camera-off rail is untouched; the cloud read is logged on every use.
