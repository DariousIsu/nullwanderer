Hybrid RAG with Reciprocal Rank Fusion merges BM25 keyword search and dense vector retrieval. RRF score = sum(1/(rank+60)) across systems — higher is better. Typical weights: BM25 0.3, dense 0.7 for factual queries; flip for keyword-sensitive tasks. Implement BM25 with Elasticsearch or SQLite FTS5 (rank() function). Retrieve top 20 from each, assign ranks, compute RRF scores, merge and sort. Final top-K fed to LLM. Hybrid consistently outperforms either alone by 5-15% on recall@10. No retraining needed — purely rank combination.
