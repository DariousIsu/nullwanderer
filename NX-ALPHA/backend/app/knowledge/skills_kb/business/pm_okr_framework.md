OKR writing framework: Objectives are qualitative, inspirational, and time-bound to the quarter. Key Results are 3-5 per objective, measurable, and calibrated to 70% achievement as the target. Avoid output KRs like "shipped X" — use outcome KRs that move a metric from A to B. Weekly check-ins track confidence percentage, blockers, and updated forecasts. Cascade from company to team to individual for alignment.
