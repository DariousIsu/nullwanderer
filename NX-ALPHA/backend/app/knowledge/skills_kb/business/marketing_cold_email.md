Cold email framework: subject line under 40 characters with a personalized hook. First line references a specific trigger such as a funding round, new hire, or product launch. Value proposition in one sentence tied to their known pain point. Single clear CTA — a 15-minute call or a direct reply. Send Tuesday or Thursday between 7-9 AM in the recipient's timezone. Follow-up sequence at day 3, day 7, and day 14. Personalization beats volume every time.
