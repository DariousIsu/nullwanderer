Scrum sprint ceremony guide: Sprint Planning runs 4 hours for a 2-week sprint — check definition of ready, confirm team capacity, break stories into tasks. Daily Standup is 15 minutes covering yesterday, today, and blockers only — no problem-solving in the standup. Sprint Review is 2 hours demoing working software to stakeholders and updating the backlog. Retrospective runs 90 minutes using Start/Stop/Continue format with one action item per person maximum.
