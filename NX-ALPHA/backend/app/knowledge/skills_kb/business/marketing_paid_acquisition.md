Paid acquisition fundamentals: target CPA equals LTV times acceptable payback period divided by months. Meta/Facebook: custom audiences built from email list, 1-2% lookalike for prospecting, 30-day visitor retargeting. Google Search: exact match for high-intent, phrase match for discovery, negative keyword list is essential. Attribution window: 7-day click and 1-day view for Meta; last-click for Google Search campaigns.
