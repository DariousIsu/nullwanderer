Statistical significance for A/B tests: p-value below 0.05 means a 5% false positive rate — not proof of an effect. Always report the confidence interval (range of plausible effects), not just the p-value. Avoid peeking: check results only at predetermined checkpoints. Use a two-tailed test for bidirectional effects. Bayesian testing (probability of being best) enables faster decisions with explicit risk tolerance for teams moving at speed.
