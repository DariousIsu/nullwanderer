A/B test experiment design: define hypothesis as "if X then Y because Z." Identify primary metric plus guardrail metrics that must never move negatively. Calculate minimum detectable effect — typically 10-20% relative lift. Use a sample size calculator such as statsig or evan-miller. Choose randomization unit carefully: user, session, or account. Avoid novelty effects by running long enough. Use holdout or interleaved design specifically for UX comparison tests.
