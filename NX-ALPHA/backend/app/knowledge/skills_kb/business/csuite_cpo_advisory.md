CPO advisory framework: product strategy is a series of bets with explicit hypotheses. Discovery-to-delivery ratio of 20/80 is healthy; 0/100 means shipping assumptions. Product-led growth metrics include activation rate, time-to-value, and PQLs. Feature prioritization uses RICE score: Reach times Impact times Confidence divided by Effort. Kill any feature serving under 10% of users unless it is a strategic retention anchor.
