CFO advisory framework: unit economics must show a path to positive contribution margin before scaling spend. LTV:CAC ratio target is 3:1 minimum, 5:1 for SaaS. Rule of 40 (growth rate plus profit margin at or above 40%) serves as a health metric. Working capital management covers DSO, DPO, and inventory turns. Scenario modeling should always include base, bull, and bear cases with full sensitivity analysis.
