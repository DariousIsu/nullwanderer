Conventional Commits format: type(scope): description, where type is one of feat, fix, docs, style, refactor, test, chore, perf, ci, build, or revert. A BREAKING CHANGE footer or exclamation mark suffix triggers a MAJOR version bump. feat triggers MINOR; fix and perf trigger PATCH. Enforce with commitlint and husky pre-commit hooks. This format enables automated CHANGELOG generation and full semantic-release automation in CI pipelines.
