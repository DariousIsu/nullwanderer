VPC Terraform module: accepts variables for cidr_block, availability zones, and private/public subnet CIDRs. Core resources: aws_vpc, aws_subnet (one per AZ per tier), aws_internet_gateway, aws_nat_gateway (one per AZ for high availability), and route tables with their associations. Outputs: vpc_id, subnet_ids, and nat_gateway_ids. Use the community terraform-aws-modules/vpc module for production-grade implementations with full feature coverage and active maintenance.
